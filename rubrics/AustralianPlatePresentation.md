| Criterion | Marks | What it assesses |
|---|---:|---|
| Application of Course Concepts to Australian Plate Evolution | 25 | Applies theoretical concepts from lectures to understand and explain specific aspects of Australian Plate structure and evolution |
| Background Research & Integration | 20 | Background research beyond lectures, synthesis with course content |
| Communication & Pedagogy | 20 | Ability to explain complex topics simply, clear teaching to peers |
| Demonstrated Understanding | 15 | Through written summary, presenter notes, and Q&A responses |
| Integration of Personal Lab/Field Experience | 10 | Integrates their own laboratory, mapping, or field work experience to provide unique insights and authentic connections to the topic |

| **Total** | **100** | |

### Application of Course Concepts to Australian Plate Evolution (25 marks)

*Applies theoretical concepts from lectures to understand and explain specific aspects of Australian Plate structure and evolution*

| Level | Points | Descriptor |
|---|---:|---|
| Exceeds | 25 | Applies course concepts effectively to the Australian Plate context. Uses appropriate examples (which may include modern tectonics, other plate settings, or direct Australian examples) to explain relevant concepts. Demonstrates clear understanding of how theoretical frameworks help interpret Australian Plate structure and evolution. |
| Mastery | 20 | Applies course concepts well to the Australian context with reasonable examples. Shows good understanding of how theoretical frameworks relate to Australian Plate evolution. May rely more heavily on either modern or historical examples but makes solid connections. |
| Sufficient | 15 | Makes basic application of course concepts to the topic with some relevant examples. Shows adequate understanding of connections between theory and Australian Plate context, though may lack depth or clarity in some areas. |
| Below | 10 | Makes weak or superficial connections between course concepts and geological settings. Struggles to demonstrate how theoretical frameworks apply to or illuminate the topic. Limited ability to show relevance to Australian Plate evolution or context. Connections between theory and examples are unclear or poorly developed. |
| No Evidence | 0 | Not addressed or contains significant misunderstandings of course concepts and their application to geological contexts. |
